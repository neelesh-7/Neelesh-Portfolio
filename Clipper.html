Clipper

Year
Aug 2026
Role
Developer
Client
Personal project
Tech stack
Python, FFmpeg, faster-whisper, FastAPI, SQLite
Status
Runs locally on one PC. Not deployed, and it never posts anything by itself.

Overview

Clipper watches live streams, finds the moments where chat and audio spike, and turns them into captioned vertical clips. A person reviews and approves every clip on a local web page before it is packaged, and the tool never posts anything itself.

Key features

  • Built a Python tool that records live Twitch and Kick streams and scores each stretch by jumps in chat speed, emote bursts and loud audio.
  • Transcribed each clip with faster-whisper and used the word timings to snap its start and end to sentence breaks.
  • Rendered vertical 1080×1920 clips with FFmpeg, adding word-by-word highlighted captions, a title card and levelled audio.
  • Kept a person in charge: a local review page to watch, trim, retitle and approve each clip, and only approved clips are packaged with a pre-flight checklist.
