Appointment Booking System

Year
Dec 2025
Role
Developer
Client
Personal project
Tech stack
Node.js, Express, MongoDB, FullCalendar

Overview

A full-stack booking system: visitors pick an open time on a live calendar and get email confirmations and reminders, and the admin manages every appointment from a dashboard.

Key features

  • Developed a booking system with a dynamic calendar, automated email confirmations and reminders, double-booking prevention and an admin dashboard.
  • Light/dark mode, anonymous public availability and drag-and-drop rescheduling.
